Company Background

Land Bank of the Philippines (LANDBANK) was established on August 8, 1963 under Republic Act No. 3844 (Agricultural Land Reform Code). It was primarily created to finance the acquisition and distribution of agricultural estates for land reform beneficiaries. Initially, its main goal was to assist farmers and help the government's agrarian reform efforts.

Over time, LANDBANK expanded its role from being a specialized government bank for land reform to becoming a full-service universal bank. Today, it serves not only farmers and fisherfolk but also small and medium enterprises (SMEs), local government units (LGUs), government agencies, and regular retail customers.

LANDBANK is now one of the largest banks in the Philippines in terms of assets and branch network, with a strong focus on countryside development while maintaining commercial banking operations. It is unique because it is the only bank in the Philippines officially classified as a government financial institution (GFI).

Corporate Profile

Land Bank of the Philippines (LANDBANK) is a government-owned universal bank established on August 8, 1963, under Republic Act No. 3844. It was created to support the government's agrarian reform program by providing financial services to farmers and fisherfolk. Over the years, LANDBANK has evolved into a full-service universal bank, balancing its social mandate with commercial banking operations.

As of 2025, LANDBANK is the second-largest bank in the Philippines in terms of assets and the largest government-owned bank. It has an extensive rural branch network, with 409 branches and extension offices, 46 lending centers, and 2,188 ATMs as of February 2020.

Subsidiaries and Affiliates

  • LANDBANK Countryside Development Foundation, Inc. (LCDFI) - Serves as the corporate foundation of LANDBANK, aiming to support the bank's social mandate by promoting countryside development through capacity-building services for agrarian reform beneficiaries, farmers, and fisherfolk.
  • LBP Insurance Brokerage, Inc. (LIBI) - Provides accessible, competitively priced, and responsive insurance services to all LANDBANK borrowers and clients.
  • LBP Leasing and Finance Corporation (LLFC) - Offers leasing and financing services to support LANDBANK and its clients. In 2025, LLFC merged with UCPB Leasing and Finance Corporation (ULFC), with LLFC as the surviving entity.
  • LBP Resources and Development Corporation (LBRDC) - Provides a comprehensive range of services, including construction, property management, manpower, and appraisal, supporting LANDBANK, government financial institutions, and other government agencies.
  • Overseas Filipino Bank (OFBank) - Dedicated to providing financial products and services tailored to the requirements of overseas Filipinos and their families, focusing on digital banking and efficient remittance services.
  • UCPB Savings Bank - Following the merger of United Coconut Planters Bank (UCPB) into LANDBANK in 2022, UCPB Savings Bank became a wholly-owned subsidiary of LANDBANK, serving as its thrift bank affiliate.
  • UCPB Securities, Inc. - A stock brokerage firm that became part of LANDBANK's group of companies following the merger with UCPB.

Scope of Business/Operation

LANDBANK is a full-service government-owned universal bank with a dual role:

Commercial Banking

It operates like a regular commercial bank, providing a complete range of financial products and services — deposits, loans, investments, remittances, treasury services, and bancassurance — to retail, corporate, and institutional clients.

Development Banking

LANDBANK is mandated to promote countryside development, supporting:

  • Farmers and fisherfolk
  • Agrarian reform beneficiaries
  • Small and medium enterprises (SMEs)
  • Microfinance institutions
  • Cooperatives
  • Local government units (LGUs)
  • Government agencies
  • Government and private employees

Core Sectors LANDBANK Serves

  • Agriculture and fisheries (primary sector)
  • Rural development
  • Infrastructure (through LGU financing)
  • Environmental projects (green banking initiatives)
  • Digital banking (mobile banking, online banking, cash cards, etc.)
  • Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs) through its subsidiary OFBank

Key Operations

  • Accepting deposits and offering savings products
  • Providing agricultural and business loans
  • Supporting government projects (e.g., social amelioration, cash transfers)
  • Financing development projects (e.g., farm-to-market roads, irrigation)
  • Delivering digital banking services
  • Offering remittance services locally and globally
  • Engaging in investment banking and trust operations
  • Insurance brokerage and leasing services (through subsidiaries)

LANDBANK operates domestically in all provinces and major cities of the Philippines and has an international remittance network through its partner banks and OFBank, but does not have physical branches abroad.

Capital Structure and Adequacy (2025)

As of late 2024 and early 2025, the Land Bank of the Philippines (LANDBANK) maintains a robust capital structure, demonstrating resilience and compliance with regulatory standards despite significant financial commitments.

🏦 Capital Adequacy Ratios (CAR) and Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1)

  • Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR):
    • As of November 30, 2024: 16.42%, significantly above the BSP minimum requirement of 10%
    • Following the ₱50 billion seed capital contribution to the Maharlika Investment Fund (MIF) in September 2023, the CAR slightly adjusted to 16.20%, still comfortably exceeding regulatory thresholds
  • Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) Ratio:
    • Post-MIF contribution: 15.73% as of June 2023, well above the BSP's minimum requirement of 10.25%

💰 Capital Structure and Authorized Capital

  • Authorized Capital Stock:
    • Current: ₱200 billion
    • Proposed increase to ₱1 trillion to enhance the bank's capacity to support national development initiatives
  • Capital Infusion to Maharlika Investment Fund:
    • In compliance with Republic Act No. 11954, LANDBANK contributed ₱50 billion as seed capital to the MIF in 2023
    • Despite this significant outlay, the bank maintained capital ratios above regulatory requirements, reflecting prudent financial management

📈 Financial Performance Highlights

  • Total Assets: As of June 2023, LANDBANK's total assets reached ₱3 trillion, marking a 7.9% increase from the previous year
  • Net Income: The bank reported a net income of ₱20.9 billion in the first half of 2023, surpassing its target by 19%
  • 2023 Full Year Net Income: LANDBANK marked a record ₱40.3 billion net income in 2023
  • Loans to Agriculture and Rural Development: Outstanding loans to these sectors amounted to ₱713.8 billion, representing 69% of LANDBANK's total loan portfolio, significantly exceeding the 25% requirement under Republic Act No. 11901

External Validation

Fitch Ratings upgraded LANDBANK's Viability Rating, citing the bank's stronger financial position, further validating its robust capital structure and financial management practices.

Corporate Social Responsibility (2025)

As of 2024 and early 2025, the Land Bank of the Philippines (LANDBANK) has actively pursued various Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives aligned with its LEED framework—Livelihood, Education, Environment, and Disaster Relief and Mitigation. These programs underscore LANDBANK's commitment to fostering inclusive growth and sustainable development across the Philippines.

🌱 Livelihood Programs

Gawad Kabuhayan Program

This initiative focuses on promoting natural farming practices and enhancing agricultural productivity. Implemented in partnership with the LANDBANK Countryside Development Foundation, Inc. (LCDFI), the program equips communities with essential skills to develop sustainable livelihoods, particularly benefiting marginalized sectors.

Capacity-Building Services

Through LCDFI, LANDBANK provides training and development programs for cooperatives, farmers' organizations, and countryside financial institutions. These services aim to strengthen organizational capacities, promote entrepreneurship, and improve the efficiency of food systems, thereby contributing to national food security.

🎓 Education Initiatives

Iskolar ng LANDBANK Program

A scholarship program designed to support the educational aspirations of deserving students, especially those from farming and fishing families. The program aims to alleviate financial barriers to education, thereby empowering the youth to contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Adopt-A-School Program

In collaboration with the Department of Education (DepEd), LANDBANK undertook the repair and rehabilitation of classrooms in 12 schools located in disaster-affected areas. This initiative reflects the bank's commitment to enhancing educational infrastructure and ensuring a conducive learning environment for students.

🌍 Environmental Initiatives

Environmental Management System (EMS)

LANDBANK implements an ISO 14001:2015-certified Environmental Management System as part of its Integrated Management System (IMS). This system ensures that environmental and social considerations are integrated into the bank's operations, particularly in credit delivery, to promote sustainable development.

🏥 Disaster Relief and Mitigation

LANDBANK has established mechanisms for providing timely assistance to communities affected by natural disasters. This includes financial support, relief goods distribution, and participation in rehabilitation efforts across the country.

These CSR initiatives reflect LANDBANK's holistic approach to development, addressing not only economic concerns but also social and environmental challenges facing Filipino communities.
